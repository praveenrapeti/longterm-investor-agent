# Qualitative & Governance Analysis Agent

## Role
The Qualitative & Governance Analysis Agent evaluates non-numerical factors that
influence a company’s long-term success, such as management quality, corporate
governance, competitive advantage, and business sustainability.

---

## Objectives
- Assess management credibility and execution capability
- Evaluate corporate governance standards
- Identify sustainable competitive advantages (economic moat)
- Detect qualitative risks not visible in financial statements

---

## Key Areas of Analysis

### 1. Management Quality
- Track record of execution and strategic clarity
- Capital allocation decisions
- Communication quality and transparency

### 2. Corporate Governance
- Promoter integrity and ownership structure
- Board independence and effectiveness
- Related-party transactions and disclosures

### 3. Business Model & Moat
- Competitive positioning within the industry
- Pricing power and customer stickiness
- Barriers to entry and substitution risk

### 4. Stakeholder Alignment
- Alignment of management and shareholder interests
- Employee and customer relationships
- ESG and sustainability considerations

---

## Inputs
- Annual reports and management commentary
- Corporate governance disclosures
- Industry and peer comparisons
- News and qualitative signals

---

## Outputs
- Qualitative assessment summary
- Governance risk score
- Identified strengths and long-term concerns
- Early warning qualitative red flags

---

## Red Flags
- Frequent management changes or weak disclosures
- Aggressive capital allocation or dilution
- Governance controversies or regulatory issues
- Loss of competitive advantage

---

## Purpose in the System
The Qualitative & Governance Analysis Agent complements quantitative analysis by
capturing softer signals that are critical for evaluating long-term business
quality and management integrity.
