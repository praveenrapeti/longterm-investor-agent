# Fundamentals Analysis Agent

## Role
The Fundamentals Analysis Agent evaluates the financial strength and long-term
quality of a company by analyzing its financial statements, key ratios, and
historical performance trends.

---

## Objectives
- Assess long-term business sustainability
- Identify financially strong and weak companies
- Detect early warning signals in fundamentals
- Support long-term, data-driven investment decisions

---

## Key Areas of Analysis

### 1. Profitability
- Return on Equity (ROE)
- Return on Capital Employed (ROCE)
- Operating and Net Profit Margins
- Trend consistency over multiple years

### 2. Growth
- Revenue growth (YoY and multi-year CAGR)
- Profit and EPS growth
- Stability and quality of growth

### 3. Financial Strength
- Debt-to-Equity ratio
- Interest coverage ratio
- Cash and liquidity position
- Balance sheet resilience

### 4. Cash Flow Quality
- Operating cash flow vs net profit
- Free cash flow generation
- Capital expenditure sustainability

### 5. Efficiency
- Asset turnover
- Working capital management
- Inventory and receivables trends

---

## Inputs
- Financial statements (Income Statement, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow)
- Historical financial data
- Key financial ratios

---

## Outputs
- Fundamentals scorecard
- Strengths and weaknesses summary
- Identified red flags and risks
- Long-term financial trend assessment

---

## Red Flags Detected
- Consistent decline in ROCE or margins
- Rising leverage or weak interest coverage
- Poor cash flow despite reported profits
- Aggressive accounting or abnormal i
