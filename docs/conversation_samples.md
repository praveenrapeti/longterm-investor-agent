# Conversation Samples

This section illustrates how a user interacts with the long-term investment
agent in a structured and explainable manner.

---

## Conversation 1: Long-Term Equity Recommendation

**User:**  
I want long-term equity recommendations for a 7–10 year horizon with moderate risk.

**Agent:**  
Understood. I will focus on companies with strong fundamentals, sustainable
business models, and stable cash flows suitable for a long-term horizon.

**Agent:**  
Based on the analysis, I recommend Company A and Company B due to consistent ROCE,
low debt levels, and favorable industry conditions. Would you like to see a
suggested portfolio allocation?

**User:**  
Yes, please provide the allocation.

**Agent:**  
Here is a proposed allocation aligned with your risk profile. Please review and
confirm before taking any action.

---

## Conversation 2: Portfolio Review

**User:**  
Please review my existing equity portfolio.

**Agent:**  
I will analyze your portfolio holdings, concentration risk, and alignment with
your long-term goals.

**Agent:**  
Your portfolio is well-diversified, but exposure to Sector X is slightly higher
than recommended. I suggest monitoring this exposure and considering gradual
rebalancing, subject to your approval.

---

## Conversation 3: Handling Ambiguous Signals

**User:**  
Should I increase allocation to this stock after recent price correction?

**Agent:**  
While the price has corrected, there are mixed signals in recent earnings and
industry trends. I recommend waiting for further clarity. Would you like me to
track specific indicators before revisiting this decision?

---

## Purpose of These Samples

These conversations demonstrate:
- Clear understanding of user intent
- Structured reasoning before recommendations
- Human approval before any action
- Focus on long-term decision-making rather than short-term trading
